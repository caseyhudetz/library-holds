Privacy policy

Library Holds for Alexa · Last updated November 24, 2026

What the skill does

Library Holds is a free Alexa skill. When you ask, it signs in to your library account and tells you which holds are ready for pickup, how many are on hold and how many are in transit.

What we collect

  • Your library card number and PIN, which you enter when you link your card.
  • Your first name, if you choose to give it, so Alexa can greet you.

We do not collect your address, email, phone number, borrowing history or anything else.

How it is protected and stored

Your card number, PIN and name are encrypted on your own device before they leave it. The encrypted result is kept by Amazon as part of your Alexa account link. Only the Library Holds skill holds the key to unlock it. We do not run a database and do not store your details on any website or server.

How it is used

Each time you ask, the skill unlocks your details for that request only, signs in to your library account, reads your holds and speaks the answer. The details are not kept after the request, shared, sold or used for anything else.

Removing your information

Unlink your account or disable the skill in the Alexa app. Amazon then deletes the encrypted link, and the skill can no longer reach your library account.

Not affiliated

Library Holds is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chicago Public Library, BiblioCommons or Amazon.
