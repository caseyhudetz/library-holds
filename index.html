Library Holds for Alexa

Link your library card

Works with Chicago Public Library cards. After this, just ask “Alexa, ask Chicago Library if my books are ready.”

Your card number and PIN stay private. They are encrypted on this device before they leave it. Only the Library Holds skill can unlock them, and only to check your holds. Nothing is stored on any website. Unlink anytime in the Alexa app.
